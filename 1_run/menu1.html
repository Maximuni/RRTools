Quick Guide
VisCheck Colors: highlights visible enemies in red and enemies behind walls in blue across Boxes, Skeletons, and 3D Chams.
Party Mark (F2): aim near a teammate in-game and press F2 to toggle them in party.txt (excluded from Aimbot).
Stream-Proof: hides both the overlay and this menu window from OBS, Discord, and screen recordings.
Mouse Calibration: run CoreRR.exe --aimtest to match your in-game sensitivity.
